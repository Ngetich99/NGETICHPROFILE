FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. I work with individuals, startups, SMEs, professionals and organizations.

Yes. Websites can be developed from scratch based on your business requirements and desired functionality.

Yes. I provide on-page SEO, keyword research, meta tag optimization, technical SEO basics, Google Search Console and Analytics setup, and local SEO guidance so your website can be found on Google.

Yes. I provide tender document organization, technical proposal preparation, company documentation and related bid-support services. Final submission remains the client's responsibility.

Yes. I provide installation and configuration support using legitimate software and appropriate licensing.

No. All pricing is negotiable. The figures shown on this site are indicative starting points, and the final quotation is agreed after reviewing your scope, timeline and budget.

Can I hire you monthly?

Yes. Monthly digital and administrative support arrangements are available depending on workload.

Yes. Many services can be completed remotely. On-site support can be arranged where practical.

Can you help me with KRA or eCitizen?
